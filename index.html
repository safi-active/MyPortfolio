I am a Software Engineering student from Goma, DRC, passionate about embedded systems and smart mining.

Inspired by the unsafe conditions artisanal miners face in Masisi, I want to develop sensor-based systems that detect hazardous conditions, provide early warnings, and improve workers’ safety.

My goal is to bridge the gap between artisanal and modern mining through automation, IoT, and practical engineering solutions that make mineral extraction safer and more efficient.

Beyond technology, I enjoy reading books and listening to hymns. I am detail-oriented, manage multiple responsibilities, and enjoy communicating ideas in public.
